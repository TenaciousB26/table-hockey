Table Hockey marker

  1. Pick a size and print this page at 100% / “Actual size” (turn off “Fit to page”).
  2. Measure the black square with a ruler. It should match the size you picked.
  3. Lay it flat on the middle of your table. The rink will be centred on it.
  4. In the game, open ⋯ More and set Marker −/+ to the printed size in cm.

Bigger is steadier from far away. If you can’t print: show this on a tablet at full brightness, lay it flat on the table, measure the black square, and enter that size in the game. Matte paper works better than glossy (less glare).
